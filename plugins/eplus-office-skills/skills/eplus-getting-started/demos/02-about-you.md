# Demo 2: tell me about you (memory)

**What it shows:** Claude can remember things about the user between
conversations, so they do not have to repeat themselves.

## Steps

1. Say: "Tell me a little about yourself and I'll remember it for next time."
   Ask in one `AskUserQuestion` (free text is always available through "Other"):
   - Your role and trade (e.g. telecom, security, electrical, project management)
   - What you are working on right now (projects or sites)
   - How you like answers (short and direct / detailed / step by step)
2. Save what they told you with Cowork's memory, in their words, as a few short
   facts ("Works as a telecom engineer on the NVA05A project", "Prefers short,
   direct answers"). Tell them exactly what you saved.
3. **If there is no memory tool in your tool list, memory is turned off for
   this account.** Say so in one sentence, do not pretend it was saved, and
   carry on: their answers are still used in demos 3 and 6 of this
   conversation.
4. Show them how to check and change it: "You can ask me what I remember about
   you, or tell me to forget something, at any time. Now and then, type
   /consolidate-memory and I'll tidy it up: merge duplicates and fix old
   facts."
5. Add the card to the cheat sheet: title "I remember you", what "Tell me about
   yourself once and I'll keep it in mind in future chats.", try "Remember
   that I ..." / "What do you remember about me?"

## Close

- What you just saw: "I saved a few facts about you so future chats start from
  them."
- Try saying: "Remember that I ..." / "Forget that I ..."
