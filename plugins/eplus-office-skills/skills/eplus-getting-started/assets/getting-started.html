Getting started with Claude

Claude works beside you in the desktop app. It writes, researches, builds documents and pages, and reaches into the systems EPLUS runs on. This page shows what to ask for and what it's connected to.

Start here

Three things to do on day one.

Connect your accounts

Open Settings, Connectors and click Connect on Microsoft 365 and the EPLUS connectors. After an app update, click Reconnect on anything that asks.

Ask in plain words

Ask the way you'd ask a colleague: "Find the spec section for firestopping and draft the RFI response." Claude works out which tools to use.

Type / for shortcuts

The / menu lists your skills and commands, like /punch-report and /export. Say "show me what you can do" any time for the tour.
