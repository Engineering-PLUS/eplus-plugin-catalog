# EPLUS Brand Guidelines Summary (Default-Fonts Edition)

> This edition of the brand summary keeps the EPLUS color palette, logo rules, and
> typographic hierarchy identical to the master guidelines, but maps every typeface to
> **universally available system fonts** (Arial and standard sans-serif fallbacks).
> Use it whenever the output must stay editable across applications that do not embed
> or support the custom company fonts — e.g. a PowerPoint exported to PDF and reopened
> in Bluebeam, AutoCAD, or another reviewer's machine. If the deliverable genuinely
> requires the embedded `Montserrat` / `Sui Generis` company fonts, use the
> `eplus-branding` skill instead.

## 1. Color Palette
Always use these exact HEX codes when styling components:
- **Primary Blue:** `#666f89`
- **Primary Green:** `#3c7d7f`
- **Dark Grey:** `#58595b`
- **Light Grey:** `#a6a6a5`
*(Note: Colors can be tinted from 100% down to 5% if needed for backgrounds/accents).*

## 2. Typography Rules (Default-Font Mapping)
The brand hierarchy is preserved exactly — only the typefaces change. The differentiation
between levels now comes from **weight, size, and case**, all of which render identically
across Windows, macOS, web, and PDF reviewers.

- **Primary font:** `Arial`.
- **Cross-application font stack:** `Arial, Helvetica, "Liberation Sans", sans-serif`.
- **Headlines:** `Arial Black` (fall back to `Arial Bold` if Black is unavailable).
- **Sub-Headlines:** `Arial Bold`.
- **Section Headings (in body):** `Arial Bold`. Title Case or ALL CAPS. Must not exceed Headline size.
- **Body Copy:** `Arial Regular` (Sentence case only).
- **Do NOT** declare, embed, or reference `Montserrat` or `Sui Generis` in this edition —
  the whole point is that text remains editable in apps that lack those fonts.

## 3. Logo Usage
- **Composition:** The logo is comprised of an "icon" (EP) and text ("ENGINEERING PLUS"). *Never* display the text without the icon.
- **Variations:**
  - Standard/Preferred: 1-color Blue logo on white/light background.
  - 1-color Black logo: For white/light backgrounds.
  - 1-color White logo: For dark backgrounds.
  - Icon Only: Specifically for digital media profile images or tight spaces.
- **Strict DON'Ts:**
  - Do NOT stretch, condense, or distort the logo.
  - Do NOT use the logo as part of a sentence or phrase.
  - Do NOT rotate the logo in any way.
